WHAT IS DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION?


At BlackBaobab, we believe in demystifying technology to the digitally-excluded so as to bridge the technology gap in Africa for organizations, and the digital skills gap for the general public. We offer simple, low-cost, low-code solutions to enable your organization to digitize your workflows at your own pace, with a tailored approach.
Digital transformation is the process of integrating digital technology into areas of your business or organization, to fundamentally change how you operate and deliver value.

Business Ecosystems

The underlying technology platform is shaped according to the needs of the organization by establishing the tools and platforms currently used.
Platforms & interactions

Usage of platform to facilitate interactions between multiple parties e.g. Google Drive to share docs.
Tech-enhanced

Technology influences the way products are built and delivered. Utilizing e-commerce platforms & incorporating customer feedback into product design.
Digitally-enhanced & tech-enabled

Technology is used for communication and distribution of products. Examples include having a website to communicate the product or integrating digital marketing into the corporate strategy
Digital assets audit

  • Audit of tools, Analysis of current digital presence ie websites + social media:
  • Development of basic website with test data and 1 functionality e.g. email funnel, social media integration.
  • Website enhancement suggestions e.g. better integration of social media.
Analysis of current workflows

  • Diagramming, MindMapping
  • Brainstorming digital solutions
  • Process automation
  •   Improved way of doing day-to-day work and handling of knowledge transfer and delegation in an organization.
  •   Security of documents and data.
  •   Easier publication of organization’s intellectual outputs eg books, brochures, informational content.
  •   Online visibility which can raise the prestige of the organization.
  •   Harness digital tools to enable more engagement with your organization by the general public & potential partners.
